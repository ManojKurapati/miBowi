Where this data comes from

Every pin is scraped from OpenStreetMap by scripts/scrape-places.js and committed as a static file — nothing is scraped in your browser, and there is no server in the loop. Each pin links back to the exact OSM record it came from, so you can check it or correct it at source.

Last refreshed —. Re-run npm run scrape to pull the latest.

Emirate labels are assigned by nearest population centre. UAE emirate borders have enclaves that this approximation will occasionally get wrong.

What is thin, and why

OpenStreetMap covers UAE pet services well but barely tags dog-friendly venues: at the last run, exactly six cafes, parks or beaches in the whole country carried a dog=yes tag. So this map is strong on vets and pet shops and weak on brunch.

Closing that gap needs a different source, which is why the scraper ships with a second provider. See the method note.

Ring ahead. Pet policies change without notice and open data lags reality. Treat this as a shortlist to phone, not a guarantee of welcome — particularly for anywhere serving food.
