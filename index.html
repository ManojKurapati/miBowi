The gap

The purchase price is the smallest number in the whole decision.

People budget for the adoption fee and the bed. The real figure is that number multiplied by twelve, then by a decade, plus one night at an emergency clinic that costs more than the animal did.

Every figure on this site is a planning estimate built from published veterinary and industry cost data, adjusted for where you live. They are not quotes, and your vet will be the final word — but they are close enough that nobody should be surprised.

What this place is

An information space, not a shop.

There is nothing to buy here and nothing to sell you. That changes what we are able to say.

We will talk you out of it

A tool that always says yes is a marketing funnel. If your answers say the timing is wrong, the result says so plainly, and then tells you which specific thing to fix and roughly how long it takes.

Welfare is part of the maths

Guinea pigs and rats are costed as pairs because keeping them singly is cruel. Flat-faced breeds carry their health burden in the score. The Scottish Fold entry tells you to buy a different cat.

Nothing is uploaded

Every calculation runs in your browser. There is no account, no tracking, no newsletter capture and no server storing what you answered. Your results are saved to this device only, so the tools can talk to each other.

Reasonable questions

Before you start

Is this going to tell me not to get a pet?
Only if your own answers say so, and only with reasons attached. Most people score in the middle — ready in principle, with two or three specific things to sort out first. That is a genuinely useful place to land, and far more common than either extreme.
Where do the cost figures come from?
They are baselines built from published veterinary pricing, pet-industry spending surveys and insurance claim data, expressed in US dollars and then adjusted twice for your region — once for the exchange rate and once for local cost of living, because a consultation in Mumbai is not a New York consultation at the spot rate. The full method, including what the model deliberately leaves out, is on the method page.
How reliable is breed matching, really?
A breed is a probability, not a promise. Breed explains a meaningful share of behaviour but nowhere near all of it — individual variation within a breed is wide, and early experience matters enormously. Use the matcher to narrow sixty-nine options down to a handful, then go and meet several actual animals. An adult rescue with a known temperament tells you more in an hour than any pedigree does.
Why are some animals costed as pairs?
Because for guinea pigs, rats, gerbils, chinchillas, budgies and rabbits, solitary housing causes real suffering, and in several countries it is illegal. Costing them singly would make them look cheaper than they honestly are and would quietly endorse keeping them that way.
Can I use this if I already have a pet?
Yes — the cost tool is just as useful for checking whether your current spending is normal or whether you are under-provisioning for the years ahead, and the matcher accounts for animals already in the house when scoring compatibility.
